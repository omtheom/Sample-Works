Their Life, in a Book
and a Film

Legacy Codex Project

The Filmmaking

Five sessions of four to five hours each, at the times and in the place that suit your father or mother. Between sessions, our crew quietly follows their day. The film then shows not only what they say, but the life they live.

The Penmanship

Every word in the book is theirs. We shape only the structure, the grammar and the flow, so it reads well on the page. The voice your family knows stays exactly as it is.

The Book

Two editions are made.

  • The family edition is for sharing with relatives and friends, with your parent’s portrait on the cover. It is printed on premium paper and bound in hardcover or by hand.
  • The archival edition is printed on museum-quality paper, made to last for generations.

The Preservation

Their story is kept in three countries, so that it outlives us all. Your family receives a preservation certificate, and any member of the family can ask to see it at any time.
